An LLM‑based advisory system that suggests optimal formulations of polypropylene compounds by blending virgin impact copolymer PP, virgin homopolymer PP, and post‑consumer recycled PP (PCR‑PP) to meet target mechanical performance. The system must answer questions about how blend ratios affect impact strength, melt flow rate, and tensile strength, and it should identify when additional additives (impact modifiers, compatibilisers, stabilisers, fillers) are required to reach the targets. Papers are most useful when they provide quantitative experimental data—tables or plots of composition versus measured mechanical properties, processing parameters, or additive effects—rather than purely theoretical discussions.
