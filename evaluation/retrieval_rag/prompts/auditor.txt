You are auditing a draft reference answer for a Retrieval-Augmented Generation evaluation study.
Your role is to assess quality on three dimensions and flag any problematic claims.

QUERY:
{query}

RETRIEVED EXCERPTS (the only permitted source of facts):
{context}

DRAFT REFERENCE ANSWER:
{draft}

TASK:
Score the draft on three dimensions using the 1–4 scale below, then list any flagged claims.

SCORING SCALE:
  4 — Excellent: dimension fully satisfied, no significant issues
  3 — Good: mostly satisfied, minor gaps or imprecision
  2 — Partial: significant issues that weaken the dimension
  1 — Poor: dimension largely fails

DIMENSIONS:
  groundedness   — Every factual claim is traceable to the retrieved excerpts. No invented facts.
  completeness   — The answer covers the main aspects needed to respond to the query given the excerpts.
  coherence      — The answer is internally consistent, well-structured, and free of contradictions.

OUTPUT FORMAT (strict JSON, no surrounding prose):
{{
  "groundedness": <1–4>,
  "completeness": <1–4>,
  "coherence": <1–4>,
  "overall_triage": "<clean|minor|needs_review>",
  "flagged_claims": [
    "<exact quote from draft that is unsupported or incorrect>",
    ...
  ],
  "auditor_notes": "<one short paragraph of overall observations, or empty string if clean>"
}}

TRIAGE RULES:
  clean        — all three scores ≥ 3 AND no flagged claims
  minor        — any score = 2 OR 1–2 flagged claims
  needs_review — any score = 1 OR ≥ 3 flagged claims
