  3: Partially useful. Addresses some aspect of the query but with significant gaps.
  4: Substantially useful. Provides relevant information that would improve the answer.
  5: Directly and comprehensively addresses the query. An ideal retrieval result.

Do NOT evaluate the chunk's quality in isolation. ONLY evaluate its utility for answering each specific query.
